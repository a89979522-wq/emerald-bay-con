03 / GOOD COMPANY STARTS WITH CARE

Be kind.
Know the boundaries.

13+ EVENT · PG-13 SPACE

Everyone deserves to feel comfortable at the bay. These guidelines apply to attendees, guests, and volunteers alike.

01

Keep it PG-13.

This event is for ages 13 and up, and minors will be present. Keep conversations, avatars, names, images, and behavior appropriate for a PG-13 space. No sexual content, explicit avatars, or graphic material.

02

Avatars are not consent.

An avatar's appearance, clothing, or attractiveness never gives permission for touching, sexual comments, photos, or attention. Ask first, respect the answer, and stop immediately when someone says no or moves away.

03

Respect personal boundaries.

Give others space. Ask before recording, taking photos, or sharing someone's information. Do not follow, pressure, or repeatedly contact someone who wants to disengage. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.

04

Enjoy the connection. Respect the person.

A friendly conversation is not a promise of friendship or private access. Respect guests' and attendees' time, privacy, and limits. Guests can step away, end a conversation, or ask staff for help with unwanted attention.

05

Make room for everyone.

No harassment, hate speech, bullying, threats, or discrimination. Avoid disruptive sounds, flashing effects, and behavior that interferes with others. Follow staff directions and practice considerate convention etiquette.

06

Speak up. We are here to help.

If something feels wrong, step away and contact event staff in the instance or through the official Discord. Do not retaliate or escalate a confrontation. Staff may remove participants whose behavior violates these guidelines.

Need support during the con? Contact event staff in VRChat or through our official Discord.
